# Data Analytics & Data Cleaning Portfolio

Welcome to my project portfolio. This repository showcases end-to-end data cleaning, preprocessing, and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) pipelines built using **Python, Pandas, and NumPy**. 

## 📂 Key Projects & Analytical Insights

* **Titanic Survival Data Pipeline (`titantic.ipynb`)**
  * **Data & Preprocessing:** Cleaned 891 passenger records by resolving missing values and structural duplicates.
  * **Key Insights:** Discovered distinct survival variances—74% of female passengers survived compared to just 22% of men. Additionally, documented class disparities where survival rates plummeted from 63% in First Class down to 26% in Third Class.

* **Product Data Cleaning Pipeline**
  * **Data & Preprocessing:** Profiled and cleaned a highly unstructured retail dataset of 4,362 product records. 
  * **Technical Fix:** Formulated data imputation strategies for records containing up to 63% missing entries, utilizing median-value imputation for numeric metrics and automated "Unknown" fallback flags for categorical values.

* **Students Performance Analysis (`StudentsPerformance.ipynb`)**
  * **Data & Preprocessing:** Analyzed test scores, demographics, and prep history across 1,000 students.
  * **Key Insights:** Verified that students who completed test preparation courses scored an average of 7.6 points higher. Identified an exceptionally strong statistical correlation (0.95) between reading and writing capabilities.

* **Health Expenditure vs Life Expectancy (`healthexp.ipynb`)**
  * **Data & Preprocessing:** Standardized, filtered, and performed time-series data alignment across 6 major nations spanning 1970 to 2020.
  * **Key Insights:** Exposed global resource anomalies—revealing that despite maintaining the highest financial spending, the USA recorded the lowest relative life expectancy at 75.8 years.

* **Blood Type Distribution Mapping**
  * **Data & Preprocessing:** Cleaned, structured, and cross-referenced international health metrics spanning 126 countries.
  * **Key Insights:** Map-profiled global type frequencies, validating O+ as the world's most dominant blood group (standing at ~40%) while mapping distinct variations across different continents.
